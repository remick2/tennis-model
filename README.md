Paper tracking only, separate from the tennis model. `.github/workflows/consensus.yml` checks hourly at :40 using the same self-restarting loop as the tennis tracker (`consensus/loop.sh`; the cron is only a backup). Each check pulls the open positions of 15 profitable Polymarket sports traders (Nov 7 roster change: Multicoloured-Serf, googleplay, ethanaz, 0xd4aa6f8e and scootergene out for BreakTheBank, purplegatto, 999585, Kyn008 and JoeyAroyyOO; picked with `consensus-screen`; Nov 8: darkumbreon88 out for ianf2026 after the health check flagged him in 50% of agreements for 5-20 directional bets/day, active most days, rarely both sides), logs games where 2+ hold $1k+ on the same side to `consensus/signals.csv` (a trader counts only on the side holding 75%+ of their money on that game), grades finished ones, and rewrites `consensus/summary.md`. A **paper play** (3+ agree, nobody in the 15 against, game not started) opens a GitHub issue. The 2-agree version lost money in the Oct 27–Nov 3 backtest, so it's logged for comparison only. Official reports open as issues: the old roster's final report Mon Nov 10, then a new-roster report every Friday from Nov 13. A daily roster health check (after 8am MT) opens a warning issue if a trader drifts toward the old problems: >17/20 directional bets a day or >33/40% of all agreements (Multicoloured-Serf), >10/15% of markets bet on both sides (ethanaz), or <5/3 bets a day (early warning/alert); each warning at most once a week. **Nov 14 audit changes:** every signal now also records the ask and Polymarket fee rate when logged and the closing price at game time; reports show units after spread + taker fees, how far the price moved our way, and a fixed go-live test for real money (30+ graded pre-game 3+ plays, positive net units, wins 2+ standard deviations above what prices implied). `consensus/audit.py` (workflow `consensus-audit`, Fridays + manual) rebuilds every signal from the traders' full trade history for a fixed panel (the Nov 3 top-50 plus later roster picks) and opens a "Consensus weekly audit" issue: do they beat the price alone, does agreement add anything, and what happened to the Nov 3 leaderboard since. No end date: it runs until the workflow is disabled in the Actions tab.
